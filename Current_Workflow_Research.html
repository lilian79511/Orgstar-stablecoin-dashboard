  • Etherscan links
  • Accountant cannot certify non-official FX rates

  • CPA has no direct chain access

Critical Workflow Gaps — What's Unsolved Today

These are the structural gaps in users' current workflows that Orgstar must close. Severity is based on frequency and intensity across all interview sessions.

🔴
No On-Chain Audit Trail
Transactions happen on public chain but no tool links them to company records, approvals, or invoice documents. Accountants cannot certify. Tax authority enforcement is imminent.
CRITICAL · All Segments
🔴
Zero Segregation of Duties
The same person who approves a payment also executes it. This is an internal control failure that disqualifies companies from Big4 audit certification and board governance standards.
CRITICAL · All Segments
🔴
FX Rate Not Locked at Timestamp
Accountants require the official exchange rate at the exact second of settlement. No tool auto-captures this from a recognized exchange. Without it, financial statements cannot be certified.
CRITICAL · Segments A + C
🟠
No Pre-Transfer Verification
Blockchain transfers are irreversible. One wrong address = permanent loss. No simulation sandbox, no address whitelist enforcement, no KYT scan before broadcast exists in current workflow.
HIGH · Segments A + C
🟠
Personal Wallet = Company Treasury
Founders and CFOs use personal MetaMask/Ledger wallets for company funds. No separation, no access control, no company-level governance. If the individual is compromised, the company is too.
HIGH · Segments B + A
🟠
No Accountant-Facing Interface
CPAs receive Excel files or Etherscan links and have to manually verify 200+ transactions per client. No read-only portal. No certified data feed. The entire audit process is manual and slow.
HIGH · External / GTM Channel
🔵
No Taiwan GL Classification
No official Taiwan GAAP/IFRS account code for stablecoins. Each accountant files differently. Creates retroactive restatement risk when FSC finally issues guidance.
MEDIUM · All Segments
🔵
Privacy vs. Compliance Tension
Clients want governance for their boards but privacy from regulators and competitors. Current tools force a binary choice. No "selective disclosure" architecture exists in any tool they're using.
MEDIUM · Segment B
🔵
No US Tax (IRS) Module
Many Taiwan founders/executives hold US green cards and have IRS global income reporting obligations. No tool tracks stablecoin cost basis or generates IRS-compatible exports. High willingness to pay.

Design Implications for the Product Team

What the workflow research means for product and UX decisions in the current sprint.

🔁
Design the Loopback Execution Flow Explicitly
The current approval flow stops at "approved." The critical design moment is what happens AFTER approval: execution authority must return to the original requester (Amy, not CFO). This anti-SoD violation mechanism is the single most important UX interaction in the entire product — design it as a distinct, unmistakable step.
Segment A + C · Must-Have in current sprint
📌
Add FX Rate as a Required Field — Even Mock
In the transaction record view, add an "FX Rate at Settlement" field that pulls from a recognized source. Even if it's not live-connected yet, building the UI field and data schema now means accountants see what they need. The schema decision made today determines if the accounting package is certifiable.
Segment A + C · Add to accounting-ready data layer
👁️
Viewer Role = Accountant Portal (Don't Build Two Things)
The "Viewer" role in the RBAC structure is the CPA read-only portal. Don't design these as separate features. Extend the Viewer role with: export button, chain verification links (TxHash links to Etherscan), and a printable audit package. This one feature activates the accountant GTM channel.
External CPA Persona · GTM-critical · Current sprint
🧪
Build a "Dry Run" Confirmation Screen Before Every Transfer
Design a mandatory confirmation step that shows: destination address, amount, estimated gas, KYT status (even if "Not checked" initially), and a 10-second countdown or explicit checkbox. This is not just safety — it's the emotional reassurance that lets Amy and David actually use the product without fear. This one screen removes the biggest psychological blocker for operators.
Segment A + C · High impact UX · Next sprint
🌗
Design for "Private First, Compliance On Demand"
For Segment B users (Kevin), the mental model is not "governance system" — it's "firewall." The product needs a mode where records exist internally but are not surfaced to external view by default. The compliance export (for board, auditor, tax) is an explicit user action, not a passive always-on data feed. This framing reduces adoption fear dramatically.
Segment B · Roadmap Phase 2 · Frame in demo narrative now
📋
Design Two Distinct Demo Scenarios for the Staging Build
The completion criteria says "1–2 enterprise scenarios." Based on the journey maps, the two highest-impact scenarios are: (1) Cross-border supplier payment with full 7-step flow from invoice to auditor export — hits Segment A, (2) Payroll batch with SoD-enforced approval showing loopback — hits Segment C. Design both flows as scripts with specific amounts and company names so every demo feels real, not generic.
