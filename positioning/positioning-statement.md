## Tagline Candidates

| Tagline | Target Audience | Tone | Possible Misread |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Web2 controls. Web3 settlement." | CFO / tech-savvy | Precise, architectural | Too technical for non-technical CFO |
| "Make your stablecoin transactions certifiable." | CFO + CPA | Outcome-focused | "Certifiable" could sound uncertain |
| "The governance layer your CPA will actually sign." | CPA-first | Pragmatic, CPA-centric | Implies existing tools are unsigned — good tension |

## Red Flags
- ⛳ "Crypto financial controls" is English-speaker-centric. Test whether Taiwan CFOs respond better to "穩定幣內控系統" or "治理平台" as Chinese-language category labels.
- ⛳ The CPA-centric pitch (tagline: "the governance layer your CPA will actually sign") works only if CPAs are already recommending products to clients. Validate CPA referral willingness before making it the lead GTM message.

## Yellow Flags
- 🟡 "Web2 controls. Web3 settlement." is strong for a technical audience but may need translation for traditional finance buyers.
- 🟡 The Segment B one-liner ("manage privately, export cleanly") may create concern about regulatory intent if taken out of context.
