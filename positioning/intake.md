## Product
**Orgstar (橙星創新科技)** — Enterprise stablecoin governance platform for B2B fintech.

Orgstar is NOT a wallet, exchange, or payment processor. It is a **governance layer** that sits above existing wallets and stablecoin holdings. It provides approval workflows, audit trails, FX timestamp certification, KYT compliance scanning, and a read-only CPA portal — enabling corporate finance teams to manage stablecoin transactions with the same internal controls as traditional banking.

Architecture: Web2 control strictness + Web3 settlement transparency ("Web 2.5").

## Problem
Corporate finance teams in Taiwan (and broader Asia) are **already moving significant sums in stablecoins** — for cross-border supplier payments, payroll, treasury management — but they're doing it through personal wallets, informal LINE message approvals, and manual screenshot records. The money moves. But they cannot:
1. Show their **CPA/accountant** a certified FX rate with official timestamp
2. Show their **CFO/board** a documented approval chain with segregation of duties
3. Show their **regulator (FSC)** KYT compliance records and an auditable transaction history

The gap is not adoption — it's legitimacy. The transactions are happening; the governance infrastructure doesn't exist.

## Target Customers
**Primary — Segment A:** Cross-border trading companies (Taiwan/Asia), USD 500K–3M monthly stablecoin volume. CFO (David) + Finance Operator (Amy). Both already using stablecoins informally.

**Secondary — Segment B:** Listed/family businesses with larger digital asset holdings (USD 5M+) needing board-level compliance. Successor generation (Kevin) bridging traditional finance and crypto.

**Secondary — Segment C:** Web3-native startups managing payroll + treasury in USDT, needing CPA-certifiable records to avoid bank freezes (Jake).

**GTM Channel:** External CPAs / blockchain accountants (Eric @ Crowe TW). 30+ stablecoin clients each. If Orgstar solves the CPA's workflow, he recommends it to all clients. White-label CPA portal is primary GTM wedge.

## Alternatives (What Customers Do Today)
1. **MetaMask + LINE approval + screenshots** — manual, unauditable, legally indefensible
2. **Personal Ledger wallet + offline Excel ledger** — no approval chain, no CPA-ready export
3. **OTC desks (informal)** — large volume, zero governance, zero documentation
4. **Gnosis Safe** — multi-sig security, but no governance UI, no FX records, no CPA portal
5. **Fireblocks** — institutional custody + policy engine, but priced for tier-1 banks ($50K+/yr), no Taiwan FSC alignment, no TWD/CPA workflow
6. **Traditional banking wire** — legitimate, but 3–5 day settlement, high fees, no stablecoin support
7. **Doing nothing / delay** — wait for regulation to clarify before formalizing
